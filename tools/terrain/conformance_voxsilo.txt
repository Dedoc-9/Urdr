# URDRVXH1 three silos, eight cells — emitted by voxsilo.py, pinned by hand, compared by the gate.
#
# `voxwork` built the ruler and proved it inert. THIS RUNG SPENDS IT.
#
# Three optimisation silos, each answering ONE question and forbidden to answer any other, measured
# ALONE, PAIRWISE and TOGETHER — the whole 2^3 lattice — under a single contract no silo may
# negotiate:
#
#     THE OBSERVABLE IS BYTE-IDENTICAL. Colour and depth, as LISTS, on every declared frame, in
#     every one of the eight cells. A silo may change its implementation and may not change what is
#     seen.
#
#     G  primitive retirement   a whole triangle skipped when it provably cannot win anywhere
#     T  tile retirement        a whole 8x8 tile skipped by testing three edges at two corners
#     A  arithmetic reduction   the edge functions stepped incrementally, no multiply per pixel
#
# THE CONTRACT CAUGHT AN UNSOUND OPTIMISATION ON ITS FIRST RUN, AND THAT IS THE FIRST RESULT.
#
# Every hierarchical-Z scheme rests on one premise: interpolated depth is a CONVEX COMBINATION of
# the vertex depths, so a triangle's nearest point is the nearest of its vertices, so a triangle
# whose nearest vertex is behind everything already in its box cannot win anywhere and may be
# dropped untested. THAT PREMISE IS FALSE FOR THIS RASTERISER:
#
#     covered pixels                          245183
#     pixels where d < min vertex depth       215300      87 in every hundred
#     worst shortfall                           5129      zmin 7783 -> d 2654
#
# THE MECHANISM IS THE TOP-LEFT FILL RULE. The interior test is `e + bias >= 0`, so the biased
# weights sum to `area + B` with B in -3..0 — and the depth still divides by `area`. The
# interpolation is therefore scaled by (area+B)/area and is NOT a convex combination, so it can fall
# BELOW every vertex of its own triangle. On a large triangle that is a rounding-level effect. On
# the witness — an area-3 sliver with B = -2 — it is a factor of one third.
#
# THE REPAIR IS ONE LINE OF ALGEBRA RATHER THAN A FUDGE FACTOR: with every weight non-negative the
# weighted sum is at least `zmin * (area + B)`, giving `zmin + (zmin*B)//area`, and that bound is
# checked against EVERY covered pixel of every walked triangle on every frame rather than trusted.
# THE NAIVE CULL IS KEPT RUNNABLE as a plant that must still MOVE the observable, because a
# refutation that cannot be executed stops being evidence the day someone edits around it.
#
# AND THE LATTICE SAYS THE OBVIOUS OPTIMISATION IS THE ONE THAT DOES NOT PAY. Reported as a PANEL
# and never fused into one number, because a divide is not a multiply is not a compare and a sum
# would invent a cost model nobody declared:
#
#     cell     walked   covered   written   multiplies
#     -        664553    245183     65893      6633411
#     T        569963    245183     65893      6493935
#     G        267485     98428     51712      3810738
#     GT       225384     98428     51712      3728136
#     A        664553    245183     65893      3139347
#     TA       569963    245183     65893      3810225
#     GA       267485     98428     51712      2396172
#     GTA      225384     98428     51712      2665590
#
# GA IS THE CHEAPEST CELL IN MULTIPLIES AND GTA IS DEARER. Adding the tile arm to GA retires 42101
# walked pixels and spends 269418 extra multiplies to do it — SIX AND A HALF MULTIPLIES PER PIXEL
# RETIRED, at a moment when arm A has already made a walked pixel cost ZERO multiplies. T is
# destructive in BOTH places it occurs, which is why both are asserted: A alone spends 3139347 and
# TA spends 3810225; GA spends 2396172 and GTA spends 2665590.
#
# AND THE VERDICT IS NOT THAT THE TILE TEST DOES NOT WORK. It retires exactly what it claims to —
# GTA walks fewer pixels than GA, and TA fewer than A — it is the EXCHANGE RATE that fails. THE
# MECHANISM IS GEOMETRY AND NOT THE TEST, and the number comes from the ruler rather than from this
# rung's own arithmetic: 664553 walked pixels across 11867 walked triangles is a MEAN BOUNDING BOX
# OF 56 AGAINST A TILE OF 64. The average triangle is smaller than one tile, so the per-tile setup
# is amortised over almost nothing. This is a verdict about 56-pixel triangles, not about
# hierarchical culling.
#
# ORTHOGONALITY on multiplies SAVED, as EXACT FRACTIONS and never as decimals, because the quantity
# decides whether two silos are redundant or destructive and a rounded one would be reporting its
# own rounding:
#
#     omega(G,T) =   56874/139476     0.41   real overlap: both retire work
#     omega(G,A) = 2079498/2822673    0.82   highly redundant
#     omega(T,A) =  810354/139476     5.81   ABOVE ONE — not redundancy, SUBTRACTION
#
# THIS RUNG MAKES NO PREDICTION CLAIM, and that is a LAW rather than a sentence. `voxproj` and
# `voxcam` pinned their predictions as DATA before their arms ran and were entitled to score them.
# The arms here ran first, so pinning a prediction now would be BACK-DATING one — the L64 class
