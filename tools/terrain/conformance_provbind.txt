# This rung resolves no legal question and could not; the surveyed surface is genuinely fragmented
# (France non-commercial panorama only, Italy authorization for cultural goods, Greece occasional

# TWO DEFECTS IN THE HANDED-DOWN DESIGN, both fatal, both one line to fix.
#  (1) THE CERTIFICATE WAS DETACHABLE. The proposed digest hashed only metadata, binding nothing to
#      the geometry. MEASURED: lifting the permissive certificate's binding from block_a and
#      presenting it with block_b's geometry gives (binding_matches=True, verdict=ADMIT) against the
#      metadata-only plant and (False, PROVBIND-UNBOUND) against the bound digest. The law is
#      H(MAGIC | cert | lattice_digest) with the lattice digest RECOMPUTED at serve time, so the
#      binding cannot be asserted by whoever supplied it. The law is stated so it can be false: it
#      requires the lift to SUCCEED against the plant AND fail against the law, so it cannot pass
#      vacuously.
#  (2) IT CONTRADICTED ITS OWN CLAIM. The stated property was "decidable at serve time from embedded
#      provenance, no external lookup" and the check then called distance_to_nearest_school() — an
#      external lookup on the hot path whose answer can differ between two serves of the same block.
#      MEASURED: the carried-field path gives one verdict across repeated serves, the live-lookup
#      plant gives more than one. The buffer test is evaluated ONCE AT CAPTURE TIME and carried as a
#      field, which makes the claim true instead of aspirational and puts the buffer inside the
#      digest's commitment.
#
