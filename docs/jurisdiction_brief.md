# Jurisdiction as a lattice predicate (URDRJUR1): a design pass

<!-- brief-falsifier: jurisdiction-law -->

`jurisdiction` is the second articulation joint read under the centrality order (after `heightfield`), and it
answers the question S11 left open — do the structural joints keep reading clean, or does one surface a boundary
law? Both, in one module. The read is a CONFIRMATION: there is nothing to correct, because the module already
did the hard epistemic work and graded it honestly. But it is the case the centrality pass hoped for — a joint
that carries a measured authority-seam law rather than merely holding still.

## OODA

**Observe.** `provbind` binds a certificate to a lattice with `H(cert | lattice_digest)` and refuses an unbound
one — but the *jurisdictional* content still lived in the certificate, as a claim about how the capture was
produced. That is metadata, and metadata is whatever the submitter says; `provbind`'s own metadata-only lift
attack is the witness that this gap was real.

**Orient.** The move, and it is the right one: "this block contains no private residence footprint" is not a
claim about provenance — it is a property of WHAT THE LATTICE IS, decidable by reading occupancy. So a
jurisdictional violation becomes a DEFECT MEASURED IN CELLS (the non-empty intersection with a forbidden region),
in the same unit and against the same canonical object the authority path already uses for collision and
visibility. A bound, truthful-*looking* certificate presented with a block sitting on the exclusion zone is
ADMITTED by metadata and REFUSED by the lattice, and the verdict is invariant under every certificate including
one asserting the opposite — a single value across all four. The defect then inherits S2's arithmetic without
re-earning it: subadditive over 55 pairs with 0 violations (53 tight, and 3 carrying defect on BOTH sides, that
last number the non-vacuity — a census of 0+0=0 would confirm any rule), and composition over prefix-disjoint
Morton subtrees is STRUCTURAL, 49 of 55 pairs, 0 exceptions — Half B's commutation restated on occupancy sets.

**Decide.** The handed-down Kleene FILTRATION — step-count `P₀ ⊂ P₁ ⊂ … ⊂ P*` as a capture-quality signature —
was tested and DID NOT SURVIVE as an integrity mechanism, and the module records both directions rather than the
convenient one. It does separate a careless doctorer (uniform-certainty captures give steps `{0,1}` against
`{1,2,3}` for boundary-ambiguous ones), but it is TOTALLY FORGEABLE: 256 of 256 filtrations are reachable by
choosing confidences, and every honest-shaped filtration is reachable by an adversary aiming at a chosen fixed
point — so the filtration is a DECLARATION BY THE SUBMITTER, the same trust structure `provbind` refused, wearing
different clothes (L22 — elegance that costs detection). `recirc` had independently found this arc's Kleene
operator idempotent (at most one step), so there is no iteration to compare. Graded honestly: the filtration is a
one-sided SCREEN with `bombtest`'s character — it catches the careless and is free to the informed — NOT the
integrity mechanism `geoquorum` needs.

**Act.** CONFIRMATION — no correction; the joint holds and its boundary is already stated. The law is enforced
live by `jurisdiction-law` (the lift attack `(True, True)`, the certificate un-consulted, subadditivity `(55, 0,
53, 3)`, structural composition `(49, 0, 55)`, admit `(0,0,0)` / refuse `(33,33,33)`), with `jurisdiction-selftest`
keeping it non-vacuous. Terrain absence drops 66 → 65.

## What this does not show (`does_not_show`)

The module states its own boundary and this brief inherits it, not upgrades it. A lattice predicate captures a
GEOMETRY, not the LAW — mapping a statute onto a cell set is a human act taken as given, and nothing here says a
surveyed boundary is correctly surveyed (the forbidden region is a pinned cell set standing in for a real
exclusion zone: DECLARED). An admissible block is not thereby otherwise honest — jurisdiction and integrity are
different predicates on the same object. And there is ZERO bound against a submitter who forges the filtration;
that number is measured at zero, not hoped to be higher. Grade: MEASURED on `jurisdiction-law` for the defect,
the refusal, the lift-attack closure, subadditivity, and structural composition; the filtration's separation AND
its total forgeability both enumerated exact, not sampled.
