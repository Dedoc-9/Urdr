# `membrane` — design brief (URDRMEM1, the semantic membrane)

**Read**: 2026-08-05, the READ pass under the lex successor selector — P46 of batch 13
(`../exe_epistemics/PREDICTIONS.md`). Outcome: **C-EQ** — which **neither** of the two pre-declared
readings named (role-reading C-FLOOR, row-reading C-INV), and which the credences priced **fourth at
12**. The joint that exposed FP-ROW's own incomplete outcome partition. Reading grade:
**CONFIRMATION**.

## What it is

**The layer that reorders work without being able to change its outcome.** An adaptive layer sitting
between a producer of obligations and the authority that discharges them can help — by presenting work
in a better order — or it can quietly become a second authority, deciding what gets handled at all.
This rung establishes which of those it is, by construction rather than by policy.

## The core law (what `membrane-law` certifies)

**Every lawful membrane produces the IDENTICAL admitted set** — decided against **nine** of them,
including a reversed order, a both-ends-interleaved adversarial order, and one that **puts a chosen
obligation last every time specifically to starve it**. So an adaptive layer changes **how efficiently
truth is reached and never what truth is**. The starvation-adversary is the load-bearing member of
that corpus: a membrane that could starve one obligation would make the admitted set depend on the
ordering, and the law is exactly that it cannot.

`membrane-selftest` refuses three plants, **each with its own name rather than tolerated**: the
*filtering* membrane DROPS obligations — named as the accelerator's characteristic failure, since
silently discarding what you cannot handle looks exactly like handling it — the *injecting* membrane
creates state, and the third likewise reddens. Naming each failure separately is what keeps "the
membrane is advisory" from being a claim nobody can check.

## The seam (P46's finding)

**"Advisory" is the CONSEQUENCE; the law is an invariance of the admitted set under reordering.** Both
pre-declared readings mis-framed it. The role-reading took *advisory* as the headline and expected the
`recirc`/`ashdepth` shape — an inherited expectation of enforcement, refused (C-FLOOR). The row-reading
expected a positive structural law about starvation-freedom (C-INV). What the row actually certifies is
that the **same quantity computed under nine different membranes agrees**, which is the shape
`commuteprop` (every order lands one head+field) and `mesh` (mesh == monolith) both resolved C-EQ.
Advisoriness *follows* from the invariance rather than being the thing proved: a layer that cannot
change the admitted set is thereby advisory, and "unable to starve" is the adversarial witness that the
invariance is non-vacuous rather than a separate law.

## does_not_show

EFFICIENCY — the law says an adaptive layer cannot change *what* is admitted; it makes no claim that
any particular ordering is faster, and wall-clock is not gated here. Nor does it show that a membrane
is USEFUL (an identity membrane is lawful and does nothing); nor that unlawful membranes are
detectable in production (the three plants are refused *by the gate*, which is not the same as a
runtime screen); nor anything about obligations outside the modelled set. A membrane that cannot alter
truth is not thereby a good scheduler. `integrity ≠ truth`.

## Falsifier

This brief cites `membrane-law`: every lawful membrane producing the identical admitted set, decided
against nine including the reversed, the both-ends-interleaved adversarial, and the deliberate
starver. If any lawful membrane ever produced a different admitted set — most sharply, if the
starvation adversary succeeded in withholding one obligation — that row reddens and this brief's
central claim dies with it.
