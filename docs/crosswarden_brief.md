# `crosswarden` — design brief (URDRWARD2, T3.25, cross-region structural anti-cheat)

**Read**: 2026-08-05, the READ pass under the lex successor selector — P39 of batch 11
(`../exe_epistemics/PREDICTIONS.md`). Outcome: **C-R**, the author's leading credence (35) correct.
Reading grade: **CONFIRMATION**.

## What it is

**The anti-cheat that only exists at the seam.** `warden` (P4) polices claimed trajectories and
positions within one authoritative field. A world cut into regions creates a class of exploit that is
invisible to any single region's warden: the cheat lives in the *boundary between* them. This rung
polices that boundary — an honest seam crossing admits, and a through-wall sprint across the seam is
`WARD-TUNNEL`.

## The core law (what `crosswarden-insufficient` certifies)

**The predecessor is provably not enough.** A shard-local warden **ADMITS both boundary exploits that
`crosswarden` refuses** — the module certifies the *necessity of its own existence* by measurement
rather than by argument. A desynced seam is `WARD-SEAM`, and the refusal battery is total and typed
(4/4 `WARD-REFUSE` sub-codes). Supporting: `crosswarden-kinematic` holds the admission law across the
seam (an honest crossing admits; an honest handoff admits **and equals the merged glide**, binding
Stage D to Stage E); `crosswarden-topological` reuses `hand.merge` — **merge == hand.merge** — and
shows β₀ RISING across the merge (B's wall becomes visible only in the merged field), so a beyond-wall
position is `WARD-UNREACH` **from the merged field alone**.

## The seam (P39's finding)

**Police across a boundary, and an insufficiency proof as the central instrument.** The warden family
had produced all three shapes before this joint — `warden` C-R, `wardhom` C-EQ, `hand` C-INV — so the
outcome was genuinely open rather than rhetorically so, and it landed on admission. The unnamed
structure is the *insufficiency proof* itself: rather than asserting that a cross-region check is
needed, the gate runs the weaker shard-local check against the same exploits and shows it admitting
them. That converts "this rung is necessary" from a design claim into a MEASURED one — the same move
`dirward` makes one rung later, which makes it a family habit rather than an isolated flourish. The
β₀-rises-across-merge detail is the quiet one: the topological evidence for the wall does not exist in
either region alone, so the certificate is genuinely a property of the merge.

## does_not_show

WHO the cheating client is, or intent (structural admission, never attribution); more than two regions
at once (the pairwise seam is the certified case; N-way is `mesh`/`nway` territory); wall-clock;
network transport of the seam handoff; anything about a client whose exploit stays entirely inside one
region (that is `warden`'s job, and this rung does not replace it). An admitted crossing is lawful
under the model, never *honest*. `integrity ≠ truth`.
