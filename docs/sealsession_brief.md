# `sealsession` — design brief (URDRSSN1, T3.56, V5 — the attested session)

**Read**: 2026-08-05, the READ pass under the lex successor selector — P55 of batch 16
(`../exe_epistemics/PREDICTIONS.md`). Outcome: **C-AB**, the author's leading credence (34) correct —
and correct *because* of a prior transferred from `meshsession` and disclosed at the freeze. Reading
grade: **CONFIRMATION**.

## What it is

**The visible world's capstone: every law of the playable arc, running as one session.** `panelight`
certified the windowed loop, `wire` equal-or-refuse replication, `ghostsnap` the actor wire. Each holds
in isolation. This rung plays whole sessions — movement, live edits with streaming, multiplayer ghost
streams — and asks whether the laws still hold *together* across a trace long enough for their
interactions to matter.

## The core law (what `sealsession-lawful` certifies)

**A genuine movement session, a wired session (live edits + streaming), and a multiplayer session
(ghost stream) each replay through the UNMODIFIED loop, wire and ghost laws to their OWN recorded
witnesses — the whole visible world attested in one trace, deterministically.** Three laws, unchanged,
composed; and the replay lands on the witnesses the session itself recorded rather than on witnesses
computed to match.

`sealsession-forge` supplies the adversarial half under the principle the mesh phase established —
**reality may not overrule the law on any axis**: a forged avatar, world or ghost witness (a session
claiming an outcome the recorded input does not produce) and **a cheater's malice-claimed edit — an
illegal edit dressed as admitted** — each refuse. `sealsession-selftest` closes the trace itself: a
tampered session refuses on its self-digest, and an anonymous session refuses on the named-host law.

## The seam (P55's finding)

**Cross-joint transfer paid here, having cost at P38 — and the ledger now has one of each.** The
freeze disclosed that P48 (`meshsession`, the *other* capstone) had resolved C-AB, and moved weight
accordingly, while pricing it only four points ahead because P38 recorded that exactly this kind of
transfer had moved a credence the wrong way (`buoyancy` → `crossing`). It paid. Transfer between
resolved joints is now 1 hurt, 1 helped — which is the honest state of that practice and not a
vindication of it.

The structural observation is that **both capstones certify composition rather than a new mechanism**,
and both phrase it identically: laws *unmodified*, composed into *one attested trace*. A capstone in
this arc is not a new law; it is the claim that the existing ones do not interfere.

## does_not_show

Sessions outside the scenes played (three playthroughs are a corpus, not a universal — L20); the
render itself (behind the D15 firewall, as everywhere in the visible phase); wall-clock or frame
pacing across a long session; that the enumerated forgeries exhaust the attack surface; network
conditions beyond those scripted. A session no law contradicts is INTEGRITY across the trace, never a
claim that the world it records is true. `integrity ≠ truth`.
