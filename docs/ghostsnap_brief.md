## What it is

**The actor wire** — equal-or-refuse ghosts. `wire` replicated the terrain; this replicates the
actors. The industry ghost-snapshot pattern streams server-authoritative entities to clients as
per-tick snapshots the client *trusts*, interpolating from bytes it cannot check. Hainuwele's
counterpart makes a ghost a **content-addressed per-tick pose record** (112 bytes: MAGIC | actor_id
| tick | parent_addr | fx | fy | facing | SHA-256) chained by parent digest — terraform's chain law
on the movement plane — admitted under the same equal-or-refuse discipline the terrain wire uses.
Not a cheaper ghost: **a ghost that cannot lie.**

## The core law (what `ghostsnap-admit` certifies)

A tampered snapshot, a foreign-parent genesis, an out-of-interest actor, and an out-of-order
snapshot each refuse with the ghost map byte-identical; a duplicate refuses (its own admission moved
the parent it binds); the in-order retry admits. Order is **structural** — no sequence numbers, the
parent chain IS the order (the genesis snapshot chains from all-zeros, the spawn). `ghostsnap-relay`
carries the equal-across-replay property; `ghostsnap-firewall` holds the interpolation/authority
split (the same D15 firewall, on ghost motion); `ghostsnap-kinematic` reuses `warden`'s step law so
a ghost's claimed motion is gated like any claimed trajectory.

## The seam (P22's finding)

Police × representation — the wire pattern's third instance (`wire`, `driftgaze`, `ghostsnap`): the
admitted object is a *claimed replica* of authoritative state, admitted only by reconstruction
under canonical law, every refusal leaving the replica byte-identical. The composition signature
holds — the module mints no new record discipline; it points terraform's chain law and wire's
admission at the actor plane.

## does_not_show

The interpolation/prediction the client renders between admitted ghosts (declared presentation
behind the firewall); wall-clock / bandwidth (`bench.py`); who computes interest (operational);
cross-placement. A ghost that cannot lie is not a ghost that is *correct* — it faithfully
replicates whatever the authority signed. `integrity ≠ truth`.
