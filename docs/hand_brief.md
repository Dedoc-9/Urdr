# `hand` — design brief (URDRHAND1, T3.23, MMO Stage D opener)

**Read**: 2026-08-04, the centrality-ordered READ pass — P23 of batch 5
(`../exe_epistemics/PREDICTIONS.md`), the batch's genuine discrimination. Outcome: **C-INV** —
handoff continuity as a structural invariant, the promoted basis's founding axis, forward. This
resolution's third scoring zero closed run 6. Reading grade: **CONFIRMATION**.

## What it is

**Seamless cross-region authority handoff**: an actor glides across a region boundary and authority
transfers between shards without a desync. **Atomic by the project's law** — a handoff is a
two-field `splice`: region A glides the prefix, region B resumes the suffix from the boundary pose
(`splice.resume`, certified memoryless). It is seamless not because two authorities blend (that
would hide float drift URDR does not have) but because the handoff pose is **bit-identical** to what
a single authority over the merged world would produce. Two shards hold fields F_A, F_B that must
have synced their scalar potential Φ in a boundary band; the canonical world is the merge (F_A west
of the split, F_B east), and the two agree across the band, so the merge is seamless.

## The core law (what `hand-equivalence` certifies)

**Handoff equivalence**: glide-prefix-over-F_A then resume-suffix-over-F_B equals `glide` over
F_merged bit-for-bit — the seam is provably invisible. Proved across two dimensions:
**latency-invariance** (bit-identical for *any* handoff tick within the band — the actor may hand
off early or late and lands on the same trajectory, so the bridge survives handoff latency) and
**scale** (`hand-batch`: one point and a whole batch crossing together each reconstruct exactly).
Non-vacuity: F_B genuinely differs from F_A east of the band, so the handoff diverges from a glide
that stayed on F_A — proving B's terrain is really used. Seam-agreement is load-bearing: a handoff
across a band where F_A ≠ F_B, or at a tick outside the band, is a typed `HAND-REFUSE`.

## The seam (P23's finding)

A structural **continuity invariant** — no-gap / no-overlap authority transfer, the
lease/migration pattern (RAN-0's exclusivity in space rather than time). B-M′ priced this
structural-invariant reading over B-A″'s admission-predicate reading and won: authority handoff is
certified by an *equality* (the seam is invisible), guarded by refusals, not by an admission gate
with continuity as a side effect. The refuse-never-blend discipline is the axis's signature —
blending is exactly what would import the drift the arc structurally lacks.

## does_not_show

Non-monotone boundary crossings (an actor that recrosses west of the band mid-suffix — this
certifies the east-crossing case); the network transport of the handoff payload; overlapping /
blended authority (rejected by construction — it imports drift); wall-clock latency and
per-handoff / batch throughput (`NOT_MEASURED` until a sealed bench). `integrity ≠ truth`.

## Falsifier

This brief cites `hand-equivalence`: handoff == merged glide, for every in-band latency tick. If a
handoff pose ever diverged from the single-authority merged glide, or the equivalence stopped
holding at some in-band tick, that row reddens and this brief's central claim dies with it.
