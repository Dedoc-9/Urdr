# `terrain_view` — design brief (URDRTVW1, T3.0, the D15 view-export firewall)

**Read**: 2026-08-05, the READ pass under the lex successor selector — P60 of batch 18, the final batch
(`../exe_epistemics/PREDICTIONS.md`). Outcome: **C-EQ**; the author priced C-INV 34 and C-EQ third at

## What it is

**The membrane between measured authority and declared presentation.** A view exists to be looked at,
tuned and iterated — none of which may be allowed to reach back into the terrain it displays. This is
the inward half of that boundary; `view_witness` (P32) is the outward half, and neither alone
suffices.

## The core law (what `terrain-view:bind` certifies)

**The view carries the recorded witness VERBATIM — bound, and subordinate.** Not a derived witness,
not a re-serialization: the authority's own bytes, carried through unchanged, so the view is
structurally a *reader* of the authority rather than a second source of it.

`terrain-view-observational` states the firewall as a measurement: **6/6 declared knobs move the view
digest, NONE moves the witness, and knob order is inert** — presentation is observational only. Both
halves are load-bearing. That the knobs move the *view* digest is the non-vacuity witness (a firewall
over knobs that do nothing certifies nothing); that none moves the *witness* is the firewall itself.
`terrain-view-selftest` makes it bite — the fold-into-witness defect diverges from the true witness, so
the firewall is demonstrably not vacuous. `terrain-view-refusal` is total and typed (4/4 `VIEW-REFUSE`:
non-hex witness · wrong length · empty · undeclared knob).

## The seam (P60's finding)

**Carrying verbatim is an identity, not an invariant — and the pair with `view_witness` is now
complete.** The prediction read "firewall" as a structural invariant (nothing flows inward) and priced
C-INV first; the central row certifies that the view's witness *equals* the recorded one. The
invariant is real and lives in the observational row, but what `:bind` asserts is an equality.

With `view_witness` read at P32, the D15 pair is closed and the shape is worth stating plainly: **one
rung proves the view cannot CONTAMINATE the authority, the other that it cannot MISQUOTE it.** An
honest one-way membrane still permits a lie about what is on the other side; an honest citation still
permits contamination. The arc needed both, built them separately, and the briefs now say so from both
ends.

## does_not_show

The RENDER — pixels stay declared and unmeasured, which is the premise rather than a limitation;
whether the view is *good*, legible or accurate to any human expectation; performance; the fifteen
presentation knobs' effects beyond the six declared ones checked here. A view bound to an authority is
a faithful quotation of it, never evidence that the authority is right. `integrity ≠ truth`.

## Falsifier

This brief cites `terrain-view:bind`: the view carrying the recorded island_sea_wide witness verbatim,
bound and subordinate. If an exported view ever carried a witness that differed from the recorded one —
the view becoming a second source rather than a reader — that row reddens and this brief's central
claim dies with it.
